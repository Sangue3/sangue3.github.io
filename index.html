Card Kit

A game-agnostic card engine. Bring your own card data, rules and art, and skin it.

  • Pure C# rules core: board, lanes, effects, turn resolution, AI what-if simulation
  • Card feel: tilt, foil, drag & drop, flips, pack opening, dissolve
  • Game shell: settings, audio hub, save/load, localization, achievements, rebinding
  • Covered by automated tests
Coming soon Unity 6 URP

RPG Core

Engine-agnostic math for RPG combat, ready to plug into your own skills and damage types.

  • Deterministic RNG for replays and saves
  • Added / increased / more modifier buckets
  • Mitigation formulas and hit rolls
  • Modifier lifecycle
