Support

Asset support

Bought a Sangue3 package and something is not working? Email prdcgomes4@gmail.com and include:

  • the package name and version (shown in the Package Manager),
  • your Unity version and render pipeline,
  • what you expected, what happened instead, and the Console error if there is one,
  • your Asset Store invoice number.

Replies usually arrive within two business days.

Before you write

Every package includes a README with setup steps and a CHANGELOG. Many problems after an update are listed there, together with the fix.

Refunds

Refunds are handled under the Unity Asset Store terms.
